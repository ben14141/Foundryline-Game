29 October 2026. Source changes only, per user instruction: do not rebuild or retest.

Starting working capital stays $4,000. An assembly station plus one two-level parts rack and one two-level finished-goods rack costs $1,900, leaving $2,100 for materials, payroll and freight when using the existing starter forklift. Extra shelf levels cost less than another full rack. Prices are centralized in EconomyTuning and used by purchase validation, charges and catalog display.

## Basis and limits

The last measured seven-rack run earned $3,360 and spent $4,985.46, including $2,864.51 wages and $725 supplier/pickup freight. At $850 per rack, the same quantities and costs would yield $5,950 revenue and $964.54 operating cash surplus. This is arithmetic using the prior run, **not a retested result**, and excludes new equipment purchases. It is not a guarantee of profit for arbitrary staffing or idle time. Existing accepted orders retain their agreed prices; the revised sale-price catalog applies to newly generated offers.

Earlier screenshots and acceptance logs predate this adjustment. The current source must be compiled on the next normal build before these prices appear in play.
