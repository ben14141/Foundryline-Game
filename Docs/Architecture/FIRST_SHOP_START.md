# First shop: plasma-cut parts

The new default map is `/Game/Foundryline/Maps/L_FirstShopStarter`. Launch directly with `Tools/Launch-FirstShop.ps1`. The empty basic shell, road-only map and larger production factory remain independent.

The starter is approximately 92 by 72 feet, with an attached open office approximately 26 by 33 feet. One plasma table is the only installed production machine. It uses the existing cut-parts recipes, manufacturing audio, animated gantry, stock consumption and server-authoritative business operations. No welding bench, assembly zone, press brake, sheet roller or demonstration tanker is supplied. The unoccupied part of the shop remains available for later purchases.

The included support equipment is one forklift, the plasma sheet-supply rack and cut-part pallet rack. Initial steel must be ordered through the office computer; contracts, scheduling, material receiving and shipment selection use the existing commerce flow. Existing starter staffing and starting capital are retained. There is no new money grant, production recipe or payroll change.

The three-monitor planning/operations desk is in the open office, away from entrance paths. Existing manual office windows, office recessed ceiling lighting, shop hanging lights, roof/skylights, Foundryline finishes and landscaping are retained. Office room partitions and automatic office furniture from the older cut-parts demonstration are omitted to keep the first shop readable.

The east shop wall has a separate 13-foot forklift garage and one tier-1 integrated small-truck shipping bay, centred about 38 feet apart. The shipping preset provides its own bollards, apron and paint. A front shop personnel door and the office's generated outside and shop-connection doors provide walking access. All remain ordinary editable placement records under the owning company/plant. Pavement shares slab grade, with separate customer parking and truck access from the frontage road. There is no raised loading ramp.

Source regression coverage: `Foundryline.Commerce.FirstShop.LayoutAndDoorClearance` checks the actual authored openings, small-truck tier, separate garage, personnel door leaves, walking-capsule clearance through the shop entrance and both vehicle openings, valid starter simulation, one production machine and no assembly work areas. `Tools/Unreal/create_first_shop_start.py` creates the separate environment map and checks four-player layout/door parity, first-person desk access, equipment restrictions and shipping approach clearance; it also captures overview, interior and shipping-door views. Runtime checks do not save company progress or test purchases.

The map and licensed presentation dependencies remain local under the existing public repository content policy. Source/build, automated collision checks and rendered multiplayer acceptance are separate evidence; see the generated `Saved/Logs/FirstShop*` reports for the most recent run.
