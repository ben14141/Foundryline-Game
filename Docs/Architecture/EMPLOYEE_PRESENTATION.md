# Employee presentation

Employees remain authoritative plant records. The roster projects existing forklift drivers and welders plus machine operators into company/plant/employee keys. A visual never creates a second simulation employee. Independent companies have independent rosters; co-op clients render the same stable IDs.

`UFoundrylineEmployeeVisualComponent` is the temporary art adapter. Its origin is the floor, forward is +X, and standing height is 180 cm. A 24 cm radius / 90 cm half-height Pawn-only query capsule is also created on dedicated servers. Seated drivers disable this capsule and use the vehicle body. Visual meshes do not affect navigation or logistics collision.

The prototype has articulated hips, knees, ankles, shoulders, elbows and wrists. Walking uses accumulated displayed distance, with a 110 cm stride, opposed feet, a planted stance, lifted swing and level boots. Wall time does not animate a stopped employee. Operating uses two-bone wrist targets at 113 cm above the floor; work phase comes from the simulation. The forklift seat was measured from the authored Blender body: cushion approximately 141 cm, cabin floor 83 cm. Its visual root attaches at X=-106 cm, with hips at 148 cm and boots on the cabin floor.

For final character art, assign a `UFoundrylineEmployeeAppearance` before initializing the component. It accepts a skeletal mesh and in-place idle, walk, operate, weld, seated and handling sequences, plus an authored foot-root/facing correction. Import at true centimetre scale. The animation adapter samples walk by distance and work by authoritative work phase; it does not add root motion or create another movement authority. The final mesh and clips must share a compatible skeleton. Missing activity clips fall back to idle. No final people models are bundled yet.

The component exposes named joint tags and `GetPresentationReport()` for native inspection. Automated checks cover inverse/forward leg contact, real motion changing pose, idle freeze, bounded component allocation, work wrist reach and actual player capsule sweeps. Native acceptance must still inspect the actual seated fit, walking and machine work under replication.


The separate editable Blender review source is `SourceAssets/Employees/Prototype/Foundryline_PrototypeEmployee.blend`, generated by `Tools/Blender/build_prototype_employee.py`. It contains four layered rigid-joint actions (Idle, Walk, Operate, Seated) and an included `ActivatePose.py` text for selecting all action slots together. Joint curves are editable; root properties record the baked reference pose values. This is source review art, not an imported skeletal character. `rig_manifest.json` records evaluated 180 cm bounds, planted walking feet and moving knees; `saved_rig_qa.json` confirms the saved file's action switch actually changes the pose. Native Unreal checks remain separate.

## Generic jobs, welding grip and bounded passing

A person hired without a job keeps the same company/plant/employee key when assigned to equipment or a welding zone, and when released. The presentation pool does not use the current role as identity. VehicleId remains a separate seat binding.

The prototype welding pose now follows the actual animated gun transform after the zone gun moves: the dominant glove palm targets the grip pivot, and the other hand supports the barrel. The palm-to-wrist offset follows the shoulder aim, including a rotated/translated business world transform. The component reports measured `weldingGripErrorCm`; inactive welding uses the machine holster. One active welder is supported per zone so employees do not stack on a shared station/tool.

`TryEmployeePassing` provides bounded directional passing inside a wide painted personnel rectangle. It adds a gradual 35 cm sidestep, parallel track and rejoin while preserving the remaining route. New segments retain 35 cm physical clearance; each actual movement step still enforces 52 cm person spacing and forklift priority. Narrow 120 cm spurs, unsuitable corners, nearby/approaching forklifts and obstructed side tracks keep waiting. This is bounded corridor passing, not unrestricted crowd avoidance. The actual two-operator simulation test proves opposing people reach equipment without teleporting or leaving the painted footprint.

The combined 111-test suite passed, including the real opposed-traffic simulation and palm alignment under an independent-business transform.
