# Public repository content

This repository publishes the Foundryline C++ project, configuration, tools and documentation. Most `Content/` and `SourceAssets/` files remain excluded, including purchased packs and wrappers that may contain licensed geometry or textures. A reviewed exception publishes original plasma-table, press-brake and sheet-roller FBX geometry, CNC artwork, manifests and native equipment assets. Reviewed original headache-rack, welding-bench/fixture, full-sheet cradle and one/two/three-level pallet-racking sources/native assets are also included; their standalone materials have no vendor dependencies. See `Architecture/EQUIPMENT_ASSET_PUBLICATION.md` and `Architecture/ORIGINAL_MODEL_ASSETS.md`. The entirely original welder/beacon and a vendor-free forklift enhancement kit are also published; complete Factory-derived forklifts remain excluded. See `Architecture/WELDER_FORKLIFT_MODEL_UPDATES.md`. The approved original V2 welding hood, fit/export metadata and uncompiled integration handoff are included as described in `Architecture/WELDING_HOOD_INTEGRATION.md`. Local files are not removed.

Purchased assets must not be redistributed through this public repository. The project currently depends on licensed Factory LowPoly, Stylized Forest/vegetation, Softline UI Sounds, Megapoly.Art truck packs and Handyman Modular low-poly stylized content, plus locally generated maps, definitions, materials and other game content. Their original Unreal package paths must be preserved.

A source-only clone is not a complete playable project. Restore the local Content and SourceAssets directories from an authorized private backup and install the required Fab licenses before running the game. Import/generation scripts in Tools document portions of setup; they are not a replacement for a complete asset backup. Builds may succeed without assets while gameplay cannot.

Generated builds, caches, logs, screenshots, local saves and IDE state are also excluded. No purchased asset source data is intentionally included in the public Git history. Unverified packed Blender texture dependencies are excluded from the equipment exception.

The original assembly collector cart, four/six/eight-compartment supply bins and wide-bay rack variants are also published, including their Blender sources, FBX exports and standalone native meshes/materials under the AssemblySupply and WideBayPalletRacking folders. Their authored materials use constant values and engine material expressions; no purchased textures or geometry are included. Collector character animation still requires the licensed Handyman pack, and forklift presentation still requires authorized local forklift assets. The latest pricing source adjustment was intentionally not rebuilt or retested.

Manufacturing Ambiences by rawAmbience is installed locally under Content/Manufacturing_Ambiences. Purchased source recordings and processed manufacturing audio under Content/Foundryline/Audio/Manufacturing and SourceAssets/Audio/ManufacturingDerived remain private and excluded from public Git. See Architecture/MANUFACTURING_AUDIO.md for mappings and restoration tools.

The original office stairs/materials, selected authored catalogue images, personnel doors, light-kit carton and office workstation sources/native files are reviewed additions documented in [PLAYTEST_ASSET_PUBLICATION.md](Architecture/PLAYTEST_ASSET_PUBLICATION.md). Purchased chairs, trucks, people, sound packs and vendor-dependent maps remain excluded.

The October 31 office assignment update also publishes the original furnishings, wall decorations and glass-door meshes/materials under `Office/Decor`, their generated Blender/FBX sources and manifest. Their authored materials use constant values or reference engine text material; native office assets reference only this game-owned decor family. The licensed Handyman clothing palette, skeleton, engineer fitting exports and fitted character assets remain private. Original open-sided rack variants retain their previously reviewed publication status. The new original open sheet-shelf Blender/FBX source is included; its local native replacement retains private legacy material assignments and is excluded. See `Features/OFFICE_ASSIGNMENTS.md` and `Planning/RACK_AND_DISPATCH_FIXES_2026-10-31.md` for gameplay and validation.
